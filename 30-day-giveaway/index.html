01

Is it free to enter?

Yes. Registering costs nothing and your trial workout is free. To qualify, register and complete your free trial session.

02

Does registering mean I'm entered?

Not yet. Registration is step one. To officially enter the giveaway, you also need to come in and complete your free trial session.

03

Is the trial workout really free?

Yes. Your trial is a free, no-obligation coached workout at our El Cajon or Clairemont location.

04

Do I need fitness or weightlifting experience?

No. Our programming serves every level, from first-timers to experienced lifters, and your coach modifies each workout to fit you.

05

What does the winner receive?

The winner gets 30 days of the full Latitude 32 experience, including unlimited team training, fitness assessments, nutrition guidance, group support, accountability and an assigned coach.

06

How do I qualify?

Register with your name, phone number and email, schedule your free trial and complete the session. Once you've completed your trial, you're officially entered.

07

Which locations can I train at?

You can do your free trial at either location: El Cajon at 776B Fletcher Parkway, El Cajon, CA 92020, or Clairemont at 5529 Clairemont Mesa Blvd, San Diego, CA 92117.

08

What if I have an injury or limitation?

Let your coach know before your trial. Our coaches can modify workouts to accommodate injuries and limitations so you can train safely.
