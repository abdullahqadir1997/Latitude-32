At Latitude 32 Fitness, we don't chase numbers—we build the foundation first. We prioritize proper movement, body awareness, and control before intensity or load. This isn't just functional fitness—it's the next evolution. We blend strength and conditioning, mobility, stability, and performance training into a well-rounded approach that actually moves the needle. You'll train with purpose, not just sweat for the sake of it.

03Our Story

Our Story

At Latitude 32 Fitness, we serve all fitness levels—whether you've been lifting for years or you've never stepped foot in a gym. Each session is led by a professional coach who guides you through a warm-up, explains the workout step-by-step, and demos what to do (and what not to do).

There's no cookie-cutter programming here. Everything is adaptable. You'll learn how to move better, build real strength, and train smart—with modifications and priorities that fit you.

It's the most affordable way to train with a strength and conditioning professional. In fact, we've been told our coaching is better than most personal training out there. This is for everybody.
