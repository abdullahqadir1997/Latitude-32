Frequently asked questions

01

Is my first workout really free?

Yes. Start by booking a free class so you can experience Latitude 32 before deciding whether you want to continue. There's nothing to pay for your first session.

02

What is the $40 for 40 Days offer?

After your free trial, you can continue with our 40 Days for $40 promotion, which includes unlimited access to our coached strength & conditioning classes for 40 days. That's just $1 a day.

03

Do I need to be in shape before I start?

No. Our classes are built for beginners, intermediate and advanced members alike, and your coach will scale or modify every workout to match your current level.

04

I've never lifted weights. Can I still join?

Absolutely. We work with people who are completely new to strength training as well as experienced lifters. Coaches demonstrate each movement and help you with technique and modifications. Our philosophy is simple: Form before Performance. Always.

05

What if I have an injury or limitation?

Let your coach know before training. Our coaches can modify workouts to accommodate injuries and limitations so you can train safely.

06

What kind of classes are included?

Coach-led strength & conditioning group classes that combine strength training, conditioning, mobility and stability work. Every workout is planned for you, so you just show up and train.

07

Where are your gyms located?

We have two San Diego County locations: El Cajon at 776B Fletcher Parkway, El Cajon, CA 92020, and Clairemont at 5529 Clairemont Mesa Blvd, San Diego, CA 92117. Both offer morning and afternoon/evening classes Monday–Thursday, plus Friday and Saturday morning sessions.

08

What happens after the 40 days?

You decide. If you'd like to keep training with Latitude 32, we offer several ongoing membership options and your coach can help you choose the one that fits your goals and schedule.
